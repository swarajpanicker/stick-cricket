How to play: you bat from behind the stumps and the bowler runs in on his own. A ring marks where the ball will meet your bat and turns green when your timing is good. Use just the four arrow keys, pressed as the ball arrives: ← leg side, → off side, ↑ lofted straight hit (can be six, but risky), ↓ safe block. Better timing, and choosing the side the ball is heading for, means more runs.
